MESSAGE FROM THE CEO

Warm greetings to everyone taking
an interest in Myanmar GSS Co., Ltd.,

and to the young people, families, employers in Japan and partner organizations who place their trust in us. When I was young, I lived in Japan for six years, working while attending school. During that time, I gained knowledge and experience not only through my studies, but also through work and everyday life. In particular, I learned the importance of responsibility, punctuality, mutual respect, earning trust and building meaningful relationships. After returning to Myanmar, I drew on the knowledge, experience and relationships I had gained in Japan to develop businesses through which I could serve our customers, help my colleagues build happy and fulfilling lives, and contribute to society. I established Myanmar GSS to put these aspirations into practice through people development and overseas employment. Having studied and worked abroad myself, I understand how important preparation is when moving to a country with a different language, culture and way of life. This is why we place great importance on preparing young people in Japanese language, workplace rules, responsibility, teamwork, Japanese culture and everyday etiquette before they leave for Japan. Through this preparation, we want young people to work with confidence in factories, elderly care facilities and other workplaces, and to become colleagues whom Japanese employers can trust and value. Helping both sides build lasting working relationships based on mutual understanding and respect is an important part of our role. My approach to business is guided by the teachings of Kazuo Inamori, whom I deeply respect and continue to learn from. In particular, I value his emphasis on caring about the welfare of others and making decisions based on what is right as a human being. We seek to put these principles into practice by treating young people, their families, employers and our colleagues with consideration, integrity and responsibility. My long-term hope is that young people who learn and gain experience in Japan will build better lives for themselves and their families. I also hope that, in time, they will apply their knowledge and professional skills in Myanmar, contributing in their respective fields to the country’s development and nation-building. We value each person’s dreams and future, and we will respond to the trust placed in us through responsible action. Together with all of you, we will work to build a future that brings lasting value to both Myanmar and Japan.

NI HTWE Chief Executive Officer Myanmar GSS Co., Ltd.
OUR PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

01
VISION

To be a trusted partner in meeting Japan’s long-term workforce needs by developing the potential of Myanmar’s young people.

Through people development, we aim to turn the knowledge, skills and experience gained in Japan into more fulfilling lives and meaningful contributions to Myanmar’s development, building a better future for both countries.






02
MISSION

① Comprehensive Preparation Before Departure

Provide preparation in Japanese language, workplace rules, safety awareness, responsibility, punctuality, reporting, communication, consultation and teamwork.We also teach Japanese culture, traditions, daily living practices, social expectations and everyday customs to help young people prepare for both work and life in Japan.


② Matching People with Suitable Workplaces

Understand each candidate’s abilities and goals alongside the needs of employers, and connect them with suitable employment opportunities. We help candidates prepare to work effectively and become trusted team members in their respective workplaces, including factories and elderly care facilities.


③ Responsible Recruitment and Collaboration

Explain employment conditions, costs and procedures clearly, while respecting workers’ dignity and rights. We work with employers and partner organizations in Japan and take responsibility for our role in supporting a smooth transition to work and daily life.


④ Learning and Growth for the Future

Encourage continuous learning, professional development and responsibility throughout each person’s employment in Japan. We aim to nurture people who can use their knowledge, skills and experience to improve their lives and contribute to Myanmar’s future development and nation-building.


⑤ Trust Built on Care for Others

Consider the perspectives of young people, their families, employers, partner organizations and our colleagues. Through integrity and mutual understanding, we build lasting relationships in which everyone involved has the opportunity to grow.
