About Myanmar GSS

Building Bridges Between Myanmar and Japan

We work closely with our partners in Japan to build lasting relationships based on trust, respect, and mutual understanding. We are committed to providing wholehearted support to our partner businesses and addressing even the smallest concerns of employers.

MESSAGE FROM THE CEO
Mr. NI HTWE Managing Director Myanmar GSS Co., Ltd.

Warm greetings to everyone taking an interest in Myanmar GSS Co., Ltd.,

Drawing from six years of living, studying, and working in Japan, the CEO gained invaluable lessons in responsibility, punctuality, mutual respect, and trust-building.

Myanmar GSS was established to leverage these experiences by developing human resources and providing ethical overseas employment opportunities.

Understanding the challenges of adapting to a new culture, the company prioritizes comprehensive pre-departure training in Japanese language, workplace culture, teamwork, and daily etiquette.

The goal is to nurture confident, trusted workers for Japanese factories and care facilities, fostering long-term relationships based on mutual respect.

Guided by the teachings of Kazuo Inamori, the company operates with integrity, human-centric values, and genuine consideration for candidates, families, employers, and partners.

Beyond personal and family upliftment for the workers, the CEO hopes candidates return to Myanmar with valuable skills to contribute directly to the nation's future development and strengthen ties between Myanmar and Japan.

OUR PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

01
VISION

To be a trusted partner in meeting Japan's long-term workforce needs by developing the potential of Myanmar's young people.

Through people development, we aim to turn the knowledge, skills and experience gained in Japan into more fulfilling lives and meaningful contributions to Myanmar's development, building a better future for both countries.

02
MISSION

Ethical Recruitment and Pre-Departure Preparation

The primary mission focuses on providing ethical, holistic preparation and recruitment for young workers moving to Japan.

By delivering comprehensive pre-departure training in Japanese language, workplace protocols, safety, and cultural expectations, candidates are equipped for smooth integration into Japanese society and workplaces.

Operating with complete transparency regarding fees and employment conditions, this mission prioritizes worker dignity, strong ethical standards, and long-term trust among candidates, families, employers, and international partner organizations.

03
GOALS

Targeted Placement and Future Development

The core goals center on precise job matching, professional empowerment, and long-term national impact.

By aligning candidates' individual skills and career aspirations with the specific needs of Japanese industries such as manufacturing and elderly care—the goal is to build highly trusted, effective team members. Beyond initial placement, these objectives emphasize continuous lifelong learning and personal development, empowering workers to improve their standard of living and ultimately apply their international experience to support Myanmar’s future nation-building.
